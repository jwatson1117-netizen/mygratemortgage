What It Is

Understanding the HELOC loan.

A HELOC — home equity line of credit — lets you borrow against the equity in your home, but with the flexibility of a credit line rather than a lump-sum loan. You're approved for a limit, then draw only what you need, when you need it, and pay interest on the balance you actually use.

Because it sits behind your existing mortgage as a second lien, a HELOC lets you tap your equity without touching the rate on your first loan — a real advantage if you locked in a low rate. It's a favorite for renovations, consolidating higher-interest debt, or keeping a flexible reserve on hand.

Locked in a great rate on your first mortgage? A HELOC lets you use your equity without giving that rate up.

Common Questions

HELOC, answered.

How is a HELOC different from a cash-out refinance?

A cash-out refinance replaces your entire first mortgage with a new, larger one. A HELOC leaves your first mortgage untouched and adds a flexible second line on top. If you have a great rate on your current loan, a HELOC lets you keep it.

Do I pay interest on the whole limit?

No — you only pay interest on the amount you've actually drawn. Your approved limit is what's available, but the balance you carry is what accrues interest.

What can I use a HELOC for?

Just about anything: home renovations, consolidating higher-interest debt, education costs, or simply keeping a reserve available. Many homeowners use it for value-adding home improvements.

How much can I borrow?

It depends on your home's value, how much you still owe, and your qualifying profile. We'll calculate your available equity and walk you through a realistic line amount.

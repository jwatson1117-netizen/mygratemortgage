What It Is

Understanding the Jumbo loan.

A jumbo loan is simply a mortgage that exceeds the conforming loan limits set each year for your county. Because these loans are larger and aren't purchased by Fannie Mae or Freddie Mac, they follow their own guidelines — and reward well-qualified borrowers.

If you're buying in a high-cost market or a luxury home, jumbo financing lets you borrow what the property actually requires without splitting into multiple loans. With strong credit and reserves, today's jumbo pricing is often surprisingly competitive.

A bigger loan doesn't have to mean a harder process — with the right file, jumbo can be as smooth as any other close.

Common Questions

Jumbo, answered.

What makes a loan 'jumbo'?

It's any mortgage larger than the conforming loan limit set for your county each year. Anything above that threshold is considered jumbo and follows its own guidelines.

Do jumbo loans have much higher rates?

Not necessarily. For well-qualified borrowers with strong credit and reserves, jumbo rates today are often very competitive with conforming loans — sometimes even lower.

How much do I need to put down?

It varies with the loan size and property, but many jumbo loans start around 10–20% down. Larger loans or investment properties may require more. We'll pinpoint it for your scenario.

I'm self-employed — can I still get a jumbo loan?

Absolutely. Jumbo underwriting regularly handles self-employed and high-net-worth borrowers, and we have documentation options built for complex income. Let's review your specifics.

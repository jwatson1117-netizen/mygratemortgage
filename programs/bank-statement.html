What It Is

Understanding the Bank Statement loan.

If you're self-employed, you know the paradox: the same write-offs that lower your tax bill also shrink the income a traditional lender sees. A perfectly successful business owner can look, on paper, like they barely qualify.

A bank statement loan fixes that. Instead of tax returns, we review 12 to 24 months of your bank deposits to estimate your real, ongoing income. It's built for business owners, contractors, and freelancers whose returns don't tell the whole story.

Your business is doing better than your tax return admits. A bank statement loan is how a lender finally sees it.

Common Questions

Bank Statement, answered.

How do you calculate my income from statements?

We total the qualifying deposits over the statement period and apply an expense factor to estimate your net income. Business accounts are often adjusted to account for overhead. The exact method depends on the program and your account type.

Personal or business bank statements — which do I use?

It depends on the program and where your income lands. Some use personal accounts, some business, and some allow a blend. Keeping business and personal banking separate makes the analysis much cleaner.

Are the rates the same as a conventional loan?

Because bank statement loans don't use traditional income documentation, they can carry different rate and down-payment requirements than conventional financing. For many self-employed buyers, qualifying on their true income is well worth it.

What else do I need besides statements?

Generally proof of self-employment (like a business license or CPA letter), consistent statements with no gaps, and clarity on which account your income flows into. We'll give you an exact checklist.

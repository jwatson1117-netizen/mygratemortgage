What It Is

Understanding the VA loan.

The VA loan is one of the most powerful home-financing benefits in the country — and one of the most underused. Guaranteed by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs, it lets those who've served buy a home with no down payment and no monthly mortgage insurance.

We are not affiliated with or endorsed by the VA or any government agency, but we're proud to help you use this benefit. Eligibility is confirmed with a Certificate of Eligibility (COE), and we'll help you request it and walk every step.

No down payment. No monthly mortgage insurance. A benefit you can use more than once in your lifetime.

Common Questions

VA, answered.

What is the VA funding fee?

It's a one-time fee that helps keep the VA loan program running for future service members. The amount varies with your down payment and whether it's your first use, and it can usually be rolled into the loan. Some borrowers — such as many receiving compensation for a service-connected disability — are exempt.

Can I use my VA loan more than once?

Yes. Your entitlement can often be restored after you sell a home and pay off the prior VA loan, and in some cases you can have more than one VA loan at a time. The rules are specific, so it's worth a conversation.

How do I prove I'm eligible?

Eligibility is documented with a Certificate of Eligibility (COE). We can help you request it — it's usually a quick step and we handle the guidance.

Is there really no down payment?

For eligible purchases up to your entitlement, yes — $0 down. It's the headline benefit of the program and one of the few true zero-down options available.
